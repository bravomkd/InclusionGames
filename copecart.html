Why we built this

Made by a teacher who didn't forget

“As a special-education teacher, I saw children with disabilities who had almost nothing made for them in the world of games and learning. No one was creating for their joy.” So we did. InclusionGames turns any computer with a webcam into a place where every child can play — children who can't hold a controller, who need more time, who learn differently. Their laughter is everything.

Simple setup

Ready in 60 seconds

No downloads. No controllers. No special device. Open the browser, show your hand, and play.

💻

Any computer + webcam

Runs in Chrome or Edge on any PC, Mac or laptop. Nothing to install.

✋

Play with your hand

The camera tracks the hand in real time. Swipe a finger to pop the right answer — no screen to touch.

🙌

Two hands to pause

Hold up two open palms to pause anytime — and again to start. No buttons, no keyboard.

⭐

Celebrate every win

Praise, stars and a friendly voice reward every correct answer — never a punishment for mistakes.

Built for inclusion

Right and safe for every child

Designed with special educators, therapists and parents. Every game has built-in accessibility — and a no-pressure, no-punishment design that protects a child's confidence.

🐢

Slow-motion mode

Slows the game and speech for children who need more processing time.

🔠

Extra-large display

Bigger targets and text for visual or fine-motor challenges.

🔊

Full voice guidance

Every instruction and praise spoken aloud — no reading required to play.

✋

Partial-hand tracking

Works with imprecise movements — a child doesn't need fine gestures to win.

🎵

Calm music option

Gentle ambient sound for children who are sensory-sensitive.

⭐

One-click special-needs mode

Activates the gentlest settings instantly for children with complex needs.

💚

No-punishment design

Mistakes are gentle. The goal is joy and confidence, not a score to fear.

🔒

Privacy first

GDPR-compliant and COPPA-safe. We don't collect personal data from children.
