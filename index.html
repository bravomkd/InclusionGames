Built for inclusion

Every setting, every child

Every game has built-in accessibility modes designed with therapists, special educators, and parents.

🐢

Slow motion mode

Bubbles and speech slowed down for children who need more processing time.

🔠

Extra large display

XL text and bubbles for children with visual impairments or fine motor challenges.

🌙

High contrast mode

High-contrast color scheme for children with visual processing difficulties.

🔊

Full voice guidance

Every instruction, letter, word and praise spoken aloud in the child's language.

🎵

Calm music option

Gentle ambient music for children who are sensory-sensitive or easily distracted.

✊

Fist to pause

Make a fist to pause anytime — no buttons to find, no keyboard needed.

⚡

1-click special needs

One button activates the optimal settings for children with complex needs.

🎯

Adjustable difficulty

3 levels: Gentle (2 bubbles), Standard (3), Challenge (4). Start where your child thrives.
